# Draft requests for data permissions

Send these from the business's own e-mail address (the owners are not incorporated: sign as the two founders of
Cointrigue).
Each request is short and specific: what we would show, to whom, and how we would credit the source. File every reply
in the company's records, and update `config/sources.yaml` (status and evidence) when permission arrives.

Adapted on 2026-10-19 from the shared platform's drafts of 2026-10-18 (TBD@ca7e44c): the product is now a paid
subscription service for a global audience.

## 1. Bitstamp — partners@bitstamp.net (the launch blocker for every crypto page)

**Subject:** Commercial-use Data License Agreement for a subscription research website

Hello,

Your API documentation directs companies that want to use Bitstamp exchange data commercially to sign a Data License
Agreement. We are building Cointrigue, an educational website about crypto markets for everyday investors, with a
free tier and paid subscriptions, for a worldwide audience. We are two founders and have no revenue yet. We would
like to:

- use daily (and later hourly) OHLC for BTC, ETH and about ten other major pairs, including the full history from 2011;
- compute and display statistics from it (moving averages, drawdowns, volatility, long-run trend lines) to free and
  paying subscribers on the website and in a weekly e-mail, always credited "Source: Bitstamp";
- not resell the raw data, offer it for download, or provide an API to it.

Could you send the agreement and its fees, tell us whether the fee depends on the number of subscribers, and whether
there is a starting rate (or a free period) for a small business before it has revenue?

Thank you, [names], Cointrigue

## 2. CoinMarketCap — written confirmation of the free Basic plan now, and of Startup before subscribing

**Subject:** Confirming commercial display rights on the Basic and Startup plans

Hello,

Your API pricing page says "Basic through Professional all include commercial use rights", including the free Basic
tier, for one product with up to 100,000 users. We are building Cointrigue, an educational website about crypto
markets with a free tier and paid subscriptions. Could you confirm in writing that the **Basic** plan allows us to:

- show the latest prices and their 1-, 7-, 30- and 90-day changes for about ten coins on the website, including to
  paying subscribers, credited "Data provided by CoinMarketCap.com" with a link;
- keep our own record of each day's closing price as we receive it, and show charts and averages computed from that
  record, for as long as we use the API.

Before we later move to the **Startup** plan, could you also confirm that it allows us to:

- store the daily history we receive (market caps, circulating supply, prices) for as long as we subscribe;
- display charts and statistics computed from it (market-cap dominance, an index of the largest coins against
  bitcoin) to free and paying subscribers of [product name], credited "Data provided by CoinMarketCap.com";
- keep showing the history we stored if we later end the subscription or move back to Basic, or tell us what must be
  removed.

Thank you, [names], Cointrigue
